# Introduction to Coordinate Systems

## Objective

Use coordinate systems to describe position, distance, direction, and movement.

## Why It Matters

Robots and drones cannot act intelligently without a frame of reference. Coordinates connect sensors, maps, cameras, and motion commands.

## Concept

In a 2D Cartesian plane, a point is written as:

$$P = (x, y)$$

The distance between two points is:

$$d = \sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2}$$

A vector from point `A` to point `B` is:

$$\vec{v} = (x_2 - x_1, y_2 - y_1)$$

## Worked Example

Point `A` is `(2, 3)` and point `B` is `(8, 11)`.

$$d = \sqrt{(8 - 2)^2 + (11 - 3)^2}$$

$$d = \sqrt{36 + 64} = 10$$
