# Signed Numbers and Order of Operations

## Objective

Handle positive and negative numbers, parentheses, exponents, multiplication, division, addition, and subtraction in the correct order.

## Why It Matters

Robots move forward and backward. Coordinates can be positive or negative. Sensors report offsets. Simulations fail when calculation order is wrong.

## Concept

A signed number includes direction or position relative to zero:

- `+5 m` can mean 5 meters forward.
- `-5 m` can mean 5 meters backward.
- `-2 C` can mean 2 degrees below zero Celsius.

Order of operations tells you which operation happens first:

1. Parentheses
2. Exponents
3. Multiplication and division from left to right
4. Addition and subtraction from left to right

For example:

$$2 + 3 \times 4 = 14$$

because multiplication happens before addition.

## Worked Example

A robot starts at `x = -3 m`, moves `+8 m`, then moves `-2 m`.

$$x = -3 + 8 - 2$$

$$x = 3m$$

It ends 3 meters to the positive side of zero.
