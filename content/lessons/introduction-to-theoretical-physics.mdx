# Introduction to Theoretical Physics

## Objective

Understand what the future theoretical physics track is for and how it connects to mathematics, computation, and physical intuition.

## Why It Matters

Advanced robotics and engineering benefit from deep math, but theoretical physics pushes the same habits further: model clearly, compute carefully, and compare ideas against reality.

## Concept

Theoretical physics uses mathematical structures to describe nature. The future track will move through classical mechanics, electromagnetism, quantum mechanics, statistical mechanics, relativity, quantum field theory, cosmology, and simulations.

One recurring pattern is the move from a quantity to its rate of change:

$$v = \frac{dx}{dt}$$

and from rate of change to dynamics:

$$F = ma$$

The later path gets more abstract, but it still depends on clear definitions, careful units, and reproducible calculations.

## Worked Example

If a cart position is:

$$x(t) = 3t^2$$

then velocity is:

$$v(t) = \frac{dx}{dt} = 6t$$

At `t = 2`, the velocity is `12` units per second.
