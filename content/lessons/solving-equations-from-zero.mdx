# Solving Equations from Zero

## Objective

Understand equations as balance statements and solve for unknowns using reversible steps.

## Why It Matters

Engineering formulas are rarely handed to you in exactly the form you need. You must rearrange formulas for voltage, current, distance, speed, time, resistance, force, and power.

## Concept

An equation says two expressions are equal:

$$left = right$$

To keep the equation true, do the same operation to both sides.

If:

$$x + 5 = 12$$

Subtract `5` from both sides:

$$x + 5 - 5 = 12 - 5$$

So:

$$x = 7$$

The goal is to isolate the unknown.

## Worked Example

Solve for current:

$$V = IR$$

Divide both sides by `R`:

$$\frac{V}{R} = \frac{IR}{R}$$

So:

$$I = \frac{V}{R}$$

If `V = 12 V` and `R = 6 ohms`:

$$I = \frac{12}{6} = 2A$$
