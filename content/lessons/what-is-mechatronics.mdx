# What Is Mechatronics?

## Objective

Explain mechatronics as the integration of mechanical systems, electronics, sensors, software, and control.

## Why It Matters

A robot arm, a drone gimbal, a CNC machine, and a smart test rig are all mechatronic systems. They only work when physical and digital parts are designed together.

## Concept

Mechatronics combines:

- Mechanical structure and motion.
- Electrical power and signal paths.
- Sensors that measure state.
- Software that interprets measurements.
- Actuators that change the physical world.
- Control loops that reduce error.

A simple control loop can be described as:

$$error = target - measured$$

The system acts to reduce that error.

## Worked Example

A thermostat is a basic mechatronic system. It senses temperature, compares it to a target, and turns heating or cooling equipment on or off.
