# Ohm's Law and Basic Circuits

## Objective

Understand the relationship between voltage, current, and resistance, then apply it to simple circuit calculations.

## Why It Matters

Robots, drones, sensors, and microcontrollers all depend on safe electrical design. Ohm's Law is the first circuit model you need.

## Concept

Ohm's Law states:

$$V = IR$$

Where:

- `V` is voltage in volts.
- `I` is current in amps.
- `R` is resistance in ohms.

The same equation can be rearranged:

$$I = \frac{V}{R}$$

$$R = \frac{V}{I}$$

## Worked Example

A `220 ohm` resistor is connected to a `5 V` supply. The current is:

$$I = \frac{5}{220}$$

$$I \approx 0.0227 A = 22.7 mA$$

That value is in the range commonly used for small indicator LEDs, though a real LED circuit also needs the LED forward voltage included.
