# Units and Dimensional Analysis

## Objective

Use units to check formulas, convert measurements, and catch calculation errors before they become engineering errors.

## Why It Matters

Units are one of the best debugging tools in engineering. If the units do not work, the formula or calculation is wrong.

## Concept

Dimensional analysis means tracking the kind of quantity each number represents.

Speed has units of distance divided by time:

$$speed = \frac{distance}{time}$$

So:

$$m/s = \frac{m}{s}$$

If you multiply speed by time:

$$\frac{m}{s} \times s = m$$

The seconds cancel, leaving meters.

## Worked Example

A robot travels at `1.5 m/s` for `12 s`.

$$distance = speed \times time$$

$$distance = 1.5\frac{m}{s} \times 12s$$

$$distance = 18m$$

The unit check confirms the result is a distance.
