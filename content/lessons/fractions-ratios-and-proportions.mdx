# Fractions, Ratios, and Proportions

## Objective

Use fractions, ratios, and proportions to compare quantities, scale designs, and solve practical technical problems.

## Why It Matters

Ratios show up everywhere: gear ratios, voltage dividers, map scale, image aspect ratio, battery capacity, sensor calibration, and drone field measurements.

## Concept

A fraction compares a part to a whole:

$$\frac{part}{whole}$$

A ratio compares one quantity to another:

$$ratio = \frac{quantity A}{quantity B}$$

A proportion says two ratios are equal:

$$\frac{a}{b} = \frac{c}{d}$$

That equality lets you solve for an unknown scale.

## Worked Example

A map says `1 cm` represents `5 m`. A path measures `8 cm` on the map. How long is the real path?

Set up a proportion:

$$\frac{1cm}{5m} = \frac{8cm}{x}$$

Since `8 cm` is eight times larger than `1 cm`, the real distance is:

$$x = 8 \times 5m = 40m$$

## Exercise

1. A gear ratio is `3:1`. If the motor turns `900 RPM`, what is the output RPM?
2. A drawing uses `1 cm = 2.5 m`. How far is `12 cm`?
3. A battery is used from `100%` to `35%`. What fraction of the battery was used?
