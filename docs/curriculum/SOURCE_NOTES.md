## Mathematical Foundations

### Arithmetic, Units, and Estimation

Primary anchors:

- NIST SI units and metric prefixes: used to ground unit-system language and prefix examples.
- OpenStax College Physics measurement chapter: used to ground accuracy, precision, uncertainty, and measured-quantity language.
- OpenStax Prealgebra arithmetic foundations: used to ground decimal place value, exponents, and scientific notation sequencing.

Curriculum intent:

- Start below algebra.
- Treat units as part of arithmetic from the beginning.
- Build estimation and reasonableness checks before calculator fluency.
- Connect basic arithmetic directly to circuits, robotics, drones, computer vision, telemetry, and physics.

### Fractions, Ratios, and Proportions

Primary anchors:

- OpenStax Prealgebra sections on ratios, rates, unit rates, proportions, and applications.
- Khan Academy Pre-algebra ratio/rate/proportion sequence, especially ratio tables, double number lines, unit rates, proportional relationships, and constants of proportionality.

Curriculum intent:

- Treat fractions as both part-whole relationships and division.
- Distinguish ratios, rates, unit rates, proportions, and proportional relationships.
- Connect ratios to gear reductions, voltage dividers, map scale, drone image scale, and physical scaling.
- Warn early that not all scaling is linear.
